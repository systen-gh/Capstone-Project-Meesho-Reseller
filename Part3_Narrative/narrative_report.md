# Recommended chart: Line chart

Reason: Revenue is measured across an ordered time period — April, May, and June 2026. A line chart clearly shows the direction and movement of revenue from month to month.

Monthly revenue trends

Revenue comparison

#=====================================================================================
Chart-Choice Justification — Three Required Questions
#======================================================================================
1. Which month had the highest total revenue?

# Recommended chart: Bar Chart

Framework: Univariate — one measure, total revenue, compared across months.
Why: A bar chart makes the highest-revenue month immediately visible and supports direct comparison between April, May, and June.
10-second message: The viewer should identify the highest-revenue month within 10 seconds.
Y-axis: Starts at zero so the visual comparison is not exaggerated.
3D: Avoid 3D effects because they can distort perceived bar heights.
Legend: Not required because there is only one series.

2. What % share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue? 24.92%

# Recommended chart: Pie Chart

Framework: Univariate / part-to-whole — Ethnic Wear's revenue share is evaluated against April's total revenue.
Why: A pie chart clearly communicates how Ethnic Wear contributes to the overall April revenue.
10-second message: The viewer should immediately understand that Ethnic Wear represents 24.92% of April's total revenue.
Y-axis: Not applicable to a pie chart.
3D: Avoid 3D pie charts because perspective can distort slice-size comparisons.
Legend: Use a legend only if multiple categories are displayed and the labels are not directly readable.

3. How do the four regions compare on total revenue?

# Recommended chart: Bar Chart

Framework: Univariate — total revenue is compared across four regional categories.
Why: A bar chart provides a clear side-by-side comparison of North, South, East, and West.
10-second message: The viewer should quickly identify the relative revenue levels of all four regions.
Y-axis: Starts at zero to maintain an accurate comparison of bar heights.
3D: Avoid 3D because it can make some regions appear larger or smaller than they actually are.
Legend: Not required when there is only one revenue series.
