PART 4 — RELIABLE REVENUE ANALYSIS AGENT
============================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of Part 4 is to create a reliable revenue
analysis agent that uses verified Growth Engine results
to generate concise, evidence-based business narratives.

The agent must communicate verified numbers safely and
must not invent causes, trends, or unsupported business facts.


2. INPUT
------------------------------------------------------------
The agent reads the verified output generated by Part 2:

Category_Wise_MoM_Growth.csv

The input contains:
- Category
- April Revenue
- May Revenue
- May Growth %
- June Revenue
- June Growth %
- May Flag
- June Flag


3. AGENT PROCESS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Load the verified Growth Engine CSV.
2. Validate the required input file.
3. Validate the required columns.
4. Identify categories flagged by the Growth Engine.
5. Generate a Context section.
6. Generate an Insight section.
7. Generate an Implication section.
8. Add validation information.
9. Save the narratives to Agent_Narratives.txt.


4. NARRATIVE STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------
Context:
Describes the verified revenue movement.

Insight:
Reports the verified revenue values and MoM percentage.

Implication:
Provides a practical management consideration without
assuming an unsupported cause.

Validation:
Confirms the category, period, revenue, growth percentage,
and flag status.
