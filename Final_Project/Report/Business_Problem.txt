BUSINESS PROBLEM
============================================================

A reseller-based e-commerce business generates a large volume
of order and revenue data across different months, regions,
resellers, and product categories. Without a structured
analytical process, it can be difficult to identify important
revenue movements, compare business performance, and communicate
findings clearly to management.

The project addresses the need for a reliable analytical
workflow that can:

- Analyze reseller order and revenue data systematically.
- Compare revenue performance across months.
- Identify significant month-on-month category movements.
- Compare revenue performance across regions.
- Identify high-spending resellers.
- Calculate relevant business performance measures.
- Present verified numerical findings in clear business language.
- Protect reseller-related information through data masking.
- Validate analytical and narrative outputs before reporting them.

The key challenge is not only to produce analytical results, but
also to ensure that the results are accurate, traceable, and
communicated without introducing unsupported explanations.

Therefore, the project combines SQL analysis, Python-based
growth calculations, reliable narrative generation, data
masking, and agent validation into a single end-to-end workflow.
