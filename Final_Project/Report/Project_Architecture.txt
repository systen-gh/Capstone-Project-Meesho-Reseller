============================================================

Each stage uses the verified output of the previous stage.

PART 1 — SQL ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

The raw order data is stored in the SQLite database
meesho.db. SQL queries are used to produce verified business
reports covering revenue, orders, categories, regions,
reseller spending, and AOV.


PART 2 — GROWTH ENGINE
------------------------------------------------------------

The Growth Engine reads verified revenue results and calculates
month-on-month growth percentages.

A threshold-based rule is then applied:

Growth > 8%       → flagged
Growth = 8%       → escalate_exact_boundary
Growth < 8%       → not_flagged


PART 3 — RELIABLE AI NARRATIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

Flagged analytical results are converted into concise business
narratives.

The narrative structure consists of:

Context
Insight
Implication

Validation checks are used to ensure that the narrative remains
consistent with the verified numerical results.

Data masking is also applied to reseller identifiers where
required.


PART 4 — REVENUE ANALYSIS AGENT
------------------------------------------------------------

The local revenue analysis agent consumes the verified Growth
Engine CSV and generates structured narratives.

The agent performs:

- Input validation
- Category identification
- Revenue comparison
- MoM growth reporting
- Narrative generation
- Output validation
- Reliability testing


DATA FLOW
------------------------------------------------------------

Raw Order Data
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Flagged Categories
      ↓
Reliable Narrative
      ↓
Revenue Analysis Agent
      ↓
Validated Business Report


RELIABILITY PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------

The architecture is designed so that downstream stages depend
on verified upstream results.

This reduces the risk of introducing unsupported figures or
business explanations during narrative generation.
