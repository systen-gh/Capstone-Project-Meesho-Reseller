EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
============================================================

This capstone project presents a data-driven revenue analysis
system for a Meesho reseller business. The project transforms
reseller order data into structured SQL reports, month-on-month
growth analysis, reliable business narratives, and a validated
revenue analysis agent.

The dataset contains 900 orders covering April, May, and June
2026, involving 24 resellers across four regions and five
product categories. SQL and Python were used to perform data
analysis, calculate revenue and performance measures, and
identify significant month-on-month category movements.

The project is organized into four major stages. Part 1 uses
SQLite and SQL queries to generate business reports such as
monthly category revenue, regional revenue, top reseller
spending, zero-order analysis, and June average order value
for delivered orders.

Part 2 introduces a Growth Engine that calculates month-on-month
revenue growth and applies a defined threshold-based flagging
rule. Part 3 focuses on reliable business narratives, data
validation, reseller ID masking, and appropriate chart selection.

Part 4 implements a local revenue analysis agent that consumes
verified Growth Engine results and generates structured
Context, Insight, Implication, and Validation sections. The
agent was tested using both positive and negative test cases.
The final validation confirmed that the expected categories,
growth percentages, and output file were correctly identified.

The project demonstrates a complete workflow from raw order data
to verified analytical output and reliable business communication.
It also emphasizes data accuracy, privacy, validation, and the
avoidance of unsupported business assumptions.
